Prompt draft example

Illustrative scene of an adult traveler listening beside a rain-streaked inn window. They wear a charcoal wool cloak over a rust-colored linen tunic. Rain traces the leaded glass while a small hearth warms the timber room. Waist-up framing, soft firelight against cool window light, reflective and calm.

This is sample text, not a message or answer from your Kin.

Your session and saved prompts are kept in this app’s own files. Each turn is a separate, one-time request. The Kin’s answer returns here; it does not appear in chat or become a memory. Only the idea and replies you enter in this session are sent. The Kin answers knowing who it is and who you are (your name and the backstory you gave it), but not your chat or its memories. The app does not read chat history or generate images. Kindroid may charge for each Kin request.
