Achievements
🏅
During a round you answer only with the buttons. Your Kin's candidate searches are sent to English Wikipedia, and its guesses are checked with Wikidata. After a missed round you can choose to type the person's name to teach the game: that name is searched on English Wikipedia, checked with Wikidata, and saved with the round's answers in this app's private learning list. Your Kin is shown that list as hints in later rounds.
