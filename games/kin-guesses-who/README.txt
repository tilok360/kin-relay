Kin Guesses Who

A twenty-questions game where your Kin guesses the real public figure you are thinking of. It asks one yes/no question at a time (up to 25) and makes a guess when it is fairly sure. Guesses are checked on Wikipedia and Wikidata, so it only names real public figures.

Two ways to play
- On the screen: Start, then answer each question with Yes, Probably, Don't know, Probably not or No.
- In chat: say "let's play Kin Guesses Who". Your Kin starts a round and asks you each question in its own words; just answer it, and it passes your answer on. It follows up by itself with the next question or its guess.

After a round
- When it guesses right, the figure is added to this app's private learning list, with the answers that led there.
- When it misses, you can tell it who it was (type the name on the screen, or tell your Kin in chat). The name is checked on Wikipedia and Wikidata before it is added.
- Your Kin is told how the round went (one short fact that lasts 3 days), so you can talk about it afterwards.
- Quit ends the round and closes the game; the learning list is kept.

Grants
- kin:read, to have your Kin choose its questions and guesses.
- net:en.wikipedia.org and net:www.wikidata.org, to search for candidates and check that a guess is a real person.

Costs
- Each question or guess is one Kin request on your account and uses Kindroid credits; a round is usually 10 to 25.
- Running time and data come from the app allowance in Kindroid's Credits. The app runs only when you answer or start a round.

Files: main.js (the game), screen/ (what you see), sample/ (what the App Library card shows).

Achievements (judged when a round ends, counted per Kin)
- Your Kin's: Mind Reader (right in ten questions or fewer), First Try (its first guess is right), Buzzer Beater
  (right after twenty or more questions), Hot Streak (right three rounds in a row).
- Yours: Stumper (your Kin can't guess), Red Herring (it makes three wrong guesses in a round), Teacher (you teach it
  a figure it missed), Master of Mystery (stump it three times).
The screen shows a seal for each and the Achievements list; your Kin hears them with the round's result and
remembers them with the round (achievements/<kin>.json).
