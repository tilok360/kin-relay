Vignette prompts work best when the most important visual details come first. Face brackets are for facial details only; use them sparingly.

Rules built into this workshop
  • Avatar Generator: one concise identity sentence, maximum 250 characters; adult age appearance, presentation, build, skin if useful, face impression, eyes, and hair. Keep clothes, jewelry, background, camera, and mood out.
  • Vignette Avatar Description: use [body: ...] for concise body details (about 150 characters maximum); use <...> sparingly for face details only; use <(ext:N)> only here; text after /// is ignored for selfies. Hair and scene details do not belong inside face brackets.
  • Vignette prompt order: art style if wanted, who is pictured, action, place, clothing, then camera, lighting, and mood. The opening details carry the most weight.
  • Names and private story lore alone may not identify a subject to the selfie engine. Describe the visible person, action, and setting directly.
  • Use clear, concrete scene language and one main framing. The native Kindroid group selfie feature combines two or three participants; that is a product feature, not a limit on every scene prompt.
  • Medieval fantasy (codex) rules: medieval fantasy style first; firelight, lamplight, sun and moon only (no lightning-like light words); period garments with two or three concrete details (fabric, fastening, trim) and leather boots or shoes; no modern clothing words; an empty place ends with “an empty scene”; a field over its cap switches to Simplified Chinese, with names in Latin letters and spoken words in English.
  • The local checker catches common wording and length issues, but cannot guarantee how the image engine will render a prompt. Change one thing at a time and keep wording that works.
