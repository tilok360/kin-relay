Vignette Prompt Workshop

A local Kindroid app for creating Avatar Generator and Avatar Description fields, building Vignette selfie scene prompts, and checking prompt text. It does not send entries to a Kin, read chat history, generate images, or require private-data/media grants. Use Kindroid's selfie flow to generate the image.

Rule sets
- Medieval fantasy (codex rules), the default: the scene builder starts with "Medieval fantasy scene" when no style is given, and the check adds: lightning-like light words flagged (use firelight, lamplight, sun or moon), modern clothing words flagged (jacket, pants, leggings, zippers, snaps, printed fabric), a wardrobe check (a period garment with at least two of fabric, fastening and trim, plus boots or shoes in full-body and wide shots), and an empty place (people: None) must end with "an empty scene" and is checked for people and body words. A field over its cap is told to switch to Simplified Chinese (names in Latin letters, spoken words in English).
- General Vignette: the original checks only.
- Avatar Generator limit: 250 characters, as Kindroid's live counter shows.
