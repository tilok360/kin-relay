Games

Drop a Kindroid app here to add it In Kindroid, open the app's menu and tap Get its code. Drag the .gz file it downloads onto this window. Nothing in it runs while it's added.

Characters

Your Kins, kept on this PC. Games ask a Kin through Kindroid's API with your key and that Kin's AI ID. Pick which Kins play each game from the game's top bar.
